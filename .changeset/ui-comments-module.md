New Comments module (`@buildpad/ui-comments`, CLI target `comments`; recipe at /comments): comments for any record. `CommentsKit` shows threads with replies at any depth (the indent shrinks per level), a comment box with bold, italic, underline, bullets and an emoji picker (Ctrl/Cmd+Enter sends, Esc cancels, Enter continues a list), @mentions, and one-click emoji reactions from a fixed list of twelve, with counts, names in the tooltip and optimistic updates that roll back on a failure. Posting, replying and reacting never blank the list: it stays on screen and is replaced quietly. Comment text is rendered as React nodes only, so a comment cannot inject markup.

The kit talks to its data through a `CommentsAdapter`. `itemsAdapter` is the default: it reads a whole thread in two requests (the top-level comments, then every reply by `root`, with the reactions nested), writes through the app's `/api/items/...` routes under the signed-in user's own permissions, and uses the column names of the `add-comments` skill (`parent`, `root`, `author`, `body`, `photos`, `created_at`; reactions with `emoji`, `reacted_by`, `target_comment`), all overridable. The skill's create hook stamps the author, the thread root and the access. Comments are immutable unless the adapter offers `edit`, `remove` or `resolve`; without `react` the chips are read-only; a viewer who may not comment sees the list and the chips only.

Strings come from the new `comments` namespace of the shared dictionary (English and Bahasa Indonesia); overrides via `BuildpadI18nProvider` or the component's `translations` prop. The CLI gains a `collaboration` category. @mentions: typing `@` searches people after a short pause (`/api/users`, or your own function), arrow keys and Enter or Tab choose, Esc closes the list; a mention is stored in the text as `@[Name](user:<uuid>)` (the add-comments skill's format), shown as a chip, and your own stands out. At most ten people per comment. The create hook decides who may be mentioned. Pictures: a picture button and paste from the clipboard (a screenshot works), PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP up to 10 MB and at most 20 per comment (SVG is refused), uploaded to `POST /api/files` and stored as file ids in `photos`, shown as thumbnails that open a viewer with previous, next and arrow keys; a picture alone is a valid comment, and Send waits while one is uploading. `itemsAdapter({ images: true })` turns it on. Notification emails ship as DaaS code in the package's `schema/` folder, not as browser code: a custom service (`comment_mail`) and two create hooks that email the people mentioned in a new comment and the author of the comment that was answered (never the author themself, never twice for one comment, skipping inactive people and addresses that cannot receive mail, every value HTML-escaped, never affecting the comment, sending nothing until `CONFIG` says who may be emailed, and keeping the commenter's address out of Reply-To unless `replyToAuthor` is set). They are pasted into a DaaS (`schema/README.md` has the steps) and were tested in a sandbox built like the DaaS's, not against a real DaaS or mail server. Not in this version: an unread marker, a Mentioned page, and a log of sent emails.
